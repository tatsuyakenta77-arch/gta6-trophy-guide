    Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough

    Built from the official trophy descriptions and PowerPyx's roadmap (55 offline trophies, 0 online, no difficulty settings, no mission gold medals, free-roam after the story, no mission replay, no New Game+). Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — there are 15 manual save slots — because there is no mission replay. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off.

    1. Step 1 — Finish the story (60–80 hours)

      Play the 5 chapters and enjoy the game. The 100% checklist is not viewable until after the story, but progress made during the story already counts. Take optional missions as they appear (you need any 100 of 200–300+ for 100%), scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals through binoculars as you go. One warning: in Chapter 1, do Lucia's balcony meditation at Jason's house — the only possibly missable trophy (see Missables).

      After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion (35 lines). It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion: scan any 200 of 277 base-model vehicles, study any 100 of 255 animals, complete any 100 optional missions, any 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, all robberies, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations, all 15 Bret target decks, all shooting ranges and drone races.     3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup (about 10 hours)

      Most trophies pop automatically during Step 2. Finish the 8 counting Strangers & Freaks strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete — see that trophy's guide), the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, Bret's hunts, the money trophies (Dime on the Dollar, Treat Yo Self, Little Boxes, On the Spot) and the one-off stunts (Catch Me If You Can, Challenging Tank, Sneakthief, Capital Offense, In The Beginning…). The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops when the last one lands.

    ⚠️ 1 potentially missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude — “Meditate at every spot across Leonida.” There are 18 meditation spots across 10 locations. The first meditation happens in Chapter 1 at Jason's house: after the main mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning; she says she is going to meditate, walks to the balcony, and you follow her and choose Accept. That balcony meditation becomes permanently unavailable after Chapter 1, and PowerPyx reports it is still unconfirmed whether it is required for the trophy. Do it in Chapter 1 to be safe. The 100% checklist only needs 15 of 18 meditations, so the checklist is safe either way. Sources: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap; HappyGamer's GTA 6 missables guide.

    Everything else is safe

    PowerPyx confirms there is no mission replay, but every other trophy-related quest giver returns after the story and all trophy content stays available in free-roam: Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret questlines, Strangers & Freaks, collectibles, activities, robberies and the whole 100% checklist. Optional missions that disappear (black icons with pink letters) do not matter for trophies — there are far more than the 100 needed. A few magazine collectibles sit in story buildings that become inaccessible, but they also spawn elsewhere and no trophy needs all of them. Time-sensitive hourglass quests can fail, but none is needed for a trophy. Keep a manual save at the start of each chapter anyway: it costs nothing and covers the one meditation above.

    Cheats: cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off for the whole run (PowerPyx).
